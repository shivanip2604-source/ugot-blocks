Criterion A is about the problem, not the product. Write about the client, their problem and why a solution is needed. Do not say what you will make or which parts you will use. In A3, study a wide range of existing products from different fields, not only ones like your own idea. You choose and design a solution later, in Criterion B.

What this criterion asks

In Criterion A you show that you understand a real problem for a real client, and that you can plan and use research to find out what the solution must do. Your Criterion A work feeds directly into the specification you write in Criterion B.
