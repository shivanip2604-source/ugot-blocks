Before you start

Orange = the part you are making now. Grey = parts you already made.

  1. Go to tinkercad.com, sign in and click Create → 3D Design.
  2. Bottom-right of the workplane set Snap grid to 1.0 mm.
  3. Click the Ruler tool (top of the shapes panel), then click the workplane to place its corner where the front-left corner of your model will be. That corner is X 0, Y 0. X goes to the right, Y goes away from you.
  4. Every shape table has Ruler X / Y / Z. X and Y are the distances from that corner to the nearest side of the shape. Z is how high the shape's lowest point sits above the workplane (black cone).
  5. Build the parts in order. Each step adds one orange part to the grey ones.
💡 If the ruler boxes do not appear, drag the shape until its edges sit on the right grid lines in the step picture (every square is 10 mm).
⚠️ Always check your numbers against the picture and the Check line. Tracking and Tracing has 15 parts in 6 steps. Expect about 90 minutes.

The steps

Tick each box when you finish a step. If you get stuck press Ctrl+Z and retry that step.

Part 1 · Build the 3D model

1Mounting plate

  1. Drag the shape from the Basic Shapes panel onto the workplane.
  2. Click it and type Size X, Y, Height (the white squares show the number boxes).
  3. If a Rotate is listed: click a curved arrow, type the angle. Picture is the judge: if it leans the wrong way type the same angle as a negative.
  4. Place it: with the Ruler at your corner type the X and Y distances, then click the black cone ▲ and type Z (height of its lowest point).
  5. Colour it grey so the next orange part stands out.

Part 2 · Check it

7Compare with the teacher's model

  1. Download UGOT_T08_CHOSEN_Line-Wing_FINAL.stl.
  2. In Tinkercad click Import → choose the file → units mm → Import.
  3. Give the imported model a different colour, select both models and use Align (L) on X, Y and Z with the middle dots.
  4. Look for anything that sticks out. A difference under about 2 mm is fine. A bigger gap means a number was typed wrongly in that part's step.
✅ Check: your whole model measures about 184.1 × 204 × 29 mm and overlaps the imported one.
Part 3 · 2D drawings and export

8Capture the three views

  1. Click FRONT on the view cube. If you can right-click the cube, switch on Orthographic. Take a screenshot.
  2. Click TOP. Screenshot.
  3. Click RIGHT. Screenshot.
  4. Paste the three screenshots on one page in Slides or Docs and crop them.

Model answer: front, top and right views with dimensions.

9Add the dimensions

10Save and export

  1. Rename the design to Line-Wing – your name.
  2. Click Export → .STL. This is the file a 3D printer slicer uses.
  3. For a laser-cut part also export .SVG (a flat outline).
  4. Upload the STL and your 2D drawing where your teacher tells you.
