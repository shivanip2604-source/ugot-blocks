Virtual UGOT Simulator: Topic 04 Joint Rescue

Your gripper arm and TOF sensor on a collapsed-building rescue table. Run your Python, watch the robot, and collect test evidence for C3.

Mission (from the Criterion B brief)

After a building collapse, light rubble must be moved without putting a person at risk. Your Claw-Reach design, built from the kit's gripper arm and TOF sensor, reaches, measures and lifts. On this table (1.2 m × 0.8 m, B1 5.2) the robot must find a model casualty or a test object, grip it, lift it and carry it to safety.

  1. Use the TOF sensor (B1 7.2, 7.3) to decide when the claw closes.
  2. Grip without crushing or shoving (B1 7.1, 6.1), then lift the arm.
  3. Carry slowly to the green safe zone and release gently.

FunctionWhat it does

  
tof_read_mm()Distance to the nearest object ahead in mm. Range 40 to 2000 mm: below 40 reads 0, above 2000 reads 2000. Each reading takes 50 ms. The sensor sits on the arm, 35 mm behind the fingertips.

  
gripper_set(position, speed=100)Move the claw to 0 (open) to 100 (closed) %. It starts moving and the program carries on. Closing takes 0.8 s at speed 100, longer when slower. At 100% with nothing inside it closes fully (6 mm pinch gap, B1 6.1).

  
button_pressed()True while the operator holds the RELEASE button.

  
time.sleep(s), time.time()Robot time, not your laptop's time.

  
print(...)Shown in the console with the robot time.

  
Only with operator assist off (your program drives the whole rescue):

  
drive_forward(speed) / drive_backward(speed)Start driving (speed 0 to 100). Keeps going until drive_stop().

  
drive_stop()Stop the wheels.

  
move_mm(mm, speed=40)Drive an exact distance (negative = backward) and wait.

  
turn_left(deg) / turn_right(deg)Turn on the spot and wait.

  
arm_set(angle)Arm servo: 0 = claw on the table, up to 80 = fully raised. Waits until it arrives. Lift at least 25° to carry.

  
odometer_mm()Wheel encoder: net distance driven along the robot's heading since the start.
