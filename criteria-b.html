How to use each exemplar

  1. Read the Design Brief and compare it with your own scenario.
  2. Study B1 and write your own measurable success criteria.
  3. Compare the three B2 ideas and invent a fourth.
  4. Check the B3 evaluation and justify your own choice.
  5. Use the B4 system diagram, flowchart, wireframe and pseudocode as a model for your own plans, then move on to Criterion C to build, code and test your robot.
